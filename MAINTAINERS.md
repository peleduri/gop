## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers come from the contributors who already do the work. If you want to become one:

1. Contribute for at least three months: merged pull requests (code, tests or docs), reviews of other people's pull requests, and help with issues and triage. The [maintainer section of the contributor ladder](CONTRIBUTING.md#maintainer) lists the requirements.
2. Say so: open an issue or a [discussion](https://github.com/peleduri/gop/discussions), or ask a maintainer. A maintainer can also nominate you.
3. The maintainers decide as described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#adding-a-maintainer), and add you to this file and to [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).

Maintainers act as individuals. Contributors from any organization are welcome, and nominations favour a maintainer group that no single organization controls.
